Your learning · Your practice · Your future

Discover what you're capable of.

Creative practice develops capabilities that matter within and beyond your discipline. Empower helps you notice them, choose what to develop, and build a record of what you've learned through making.

Choose a direction

Explore 39 skills across five dimensions, then choose up to three to develop.

Connect your work

Name a creative project and record what you're trying to achieve.

Show your learning

Add evidence and reflection, then export a student-owned record.

Step 1 · Explore

What would you like to develop?

Choose up to three skills. There's no score, ranking or correct answer, and you can return to change your choices.

Your current priorities: None selected yet
Step 2 · Connect to practice

My creative project

A real assignment, self-directed piece, group project or other creative activity can help you notice how skills develop.

Step 3 · Notice, evidence, reflect

What changed through your project?

You can return at the beginning, during or after your project. For each priority, describe a starting point, a real example, and what you learned. There is no numerical self-rating.

Step 4 · Your portable record

My direction

This is a draft record to help you describe learning through creative practice — not a certificate or assessed outcome. You can save it as a text file, export structured data, or print to PDF.

Testing the demonstration

For the pilot planning stage, you can download a feedback form and use it separately. Do not enter personal information about students into this prototype.
